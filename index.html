⚠️ First Time Setup: Enable USB Debugging

Step 1: Enable Developer Options

  1. Open Settings on your Android phone
  2. Go to About Phone (usually at the bottom)
  3. Find Build Number (might be under "Software Information")
  4. Tap Build Number 7 times quickly
  5. You'll see "You are now a developer!" message

Step 2: Turn On USB Debugging

  1. Go back to main Settings
  2. Look for Developer Options (near bottom or in System)
  3. Find USB Debugging and toggle it ON
  4. Confirm "Allow USB debugging" if prompted

Step 3: Connect & Authorize

  1. Plug your phone into your computer with a USB cable
  2. Click the "Connect Android Device" button above
  3. Select your device from the popup
  4. Important: Look at your phone screen! A popup will ask to "Allow USB debugging"
  5. Check "Always allow from this computer" and tap OK

Troubleshooting:

  • 🔌 Connection failed? Try a different USB cable or USB port
  • 📱 Device not showing up? Make sure USB mode is set to "File Transfer" or "MTP", not "Charging only"
  • 🔧 Interface busy error? Open terminal/command prompt and run: adb kill-server
  • 🔄 Still not working? Disconnect phone, restart both devices, and try again
